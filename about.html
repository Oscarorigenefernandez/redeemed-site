THE STORY BEHIND THE NAME

MORE THAN
A LABEL.

Redeemed is a purpose-driven clothing and lifestyle brand built around faith, identity, and the choice to live with intention.

OUR FOUNDATION

ROOTED IN FAITH.
OPEN TO EVERYONE.

Faith is part of our foundation, but Redeemed is made to welcome everyone. We believe what you wear can be an expression of what matters to you: confidence, growth, purpose, and identity. Our goal is to create thoughtful everyday pieces with a meaning that reaches beyond the clothing itself.

The name “Redeemed” represents a reminder that your past does not define your future, that growth is always possible, and that there is more to life than a label.
